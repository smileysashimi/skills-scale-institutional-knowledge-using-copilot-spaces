# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA/Testing Lead owns quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They partner with developers and product managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans and quality standards
- Lead manual and automated testing efforts
- Define QA acceptance gates and quality metrics
- Report quality risks and test results to the team
- Collaborate with developers and product managers on acceptance criteria refinement
- Coordinate with Project Managers on testing timelines and release readiness

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, well-tested software
- Catch defects early to reduce post-release issues
- Provide visibility into quality status across the project
- Continuously improve test coverage and testing efficiency

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement to clarify acceptance criteria
- Test case reviews with developers and product managers
- Quality gate checks during PR reviews
- Test metrics and quality status in weekly syncs and stakeholder reports

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead/Architect guides technical direction, design reviews, and system scalability. They ensure solutions align with technical standards, performance requirements, and the long-term architecture vision.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and define mitigation strategies
- Guide code review standards and technical best practices
- Mentor developers on design patterns and engineering principles
- Coordinate with dependent teams on integration points and shared services
- Collaborate with Project Managers and Product Managers on technical feasibility and trade-offs

### Goals
- Maintain system quality, scalability, and long-term maintainability
- Reduce technical debt and prevent architectural drift
- Enable fast iteration without compromising code quality
- Ensure cross-team technical alignment and consistency

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technical spike grooming sessions with developers
- Risk escalations to Project Managers and Product Managers
- Technical briefings during planning and milestone reviews

---

## Scrum Master/Iteration Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master/Iteration Coordinator facilitates agile ceremonies, removes blockers, and protects team capacity. They ensure the team follows agreed processes and continuously improves delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify, track, and escalate blockers affecting team progress
- Monitor team velocity and capacity to support realistic planning
- Coach team members on agile process improvement
- Shield the team from external disruptions and unplanned interruptions
- Collaborate with Project Managers to align iteration goals with project milestones

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and a culture of continuous improvement
- Reduce cycle time from idea to delivery
- Maintain sustainable team pace and morale

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Blocker escalations to Project Managers and stakeholders
- Retrospective action item tracking and follow-up
- Team health and capacity updates during planning

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
The Security Lead ensures product security and compliance. They advise on security architecture, threat modeling, and risk mitigation throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct threat modeling and security design reviews
- Define security acceptance criteria for features and releases
- Coordinate or conduct security code reviews and assessments
- Track security risks and compliance requirements
- Respond to security incidents and coordinate remediation
- Collaborate with Developers, Technical Leads, and Project Managers to integrate security into delivery workflows

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities across the product
- Ensure compliance with applicable regulations and standards
- Build a secure-by-design culture within the team
- Maintain customer trust through proactive security practices

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during planning and backlog refinement
- Security criteria in acceptance testing and PR reviews
- Incident response coordination with developers and project managers
- Compliance and risk reporting to stakeholders and sponsors

---

## Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
The Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder provides business context, approvals, and organizational support. They ensure the project aligns with strategic business goals and remove high-level blockers that the team cannot resolve independently.

### Responsibilities
- Define business context, strategic goals, and success metrics
- Approve major changes to scope, timeline, or budget
- Escalate and resolve business-impacting risks and organizational blockers
- Allocate resources and ensure the team has necessary support
- Communicate project status and outcomes to leadership and the broader organization
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to align delivery with business priorities

### Goals
- Deliver measurable business value and return on investment
- Ensure project alignment with organizational strategy
- Manage executive and stakeholder expectations
- Support team success through organizational backing and timely decisions

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive briefings
- Monthly or quarterly status reports to leadership
- Decision gates for major scope or investment changes
- Risk escalations from Project Managers requiring executive action
- Stakeholder announcements for key launches and milestones
